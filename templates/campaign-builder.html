Step 2b · Market research

Learn from what's already winning

Instead of guessing, look at the ads competitors are running for properties like yours — then build something sharper. Three simple steps, no marketing background needed.

  1. Find — tap the buttons below to open the ads competitors are running near you.
  2. Teardown — paste one competitor ad (or a screenshot) and the app explains why it works and writes you a stronger version.
  3. Angle — get the one message most brokers in your area are missing, so you stand out.
A quick honest note: Facebook and Instagram keep likes, views and “save rates” private, so no tool — this one included — can secretly pull a rival's real numbers. What this tab does instead is take you straight to the public ads brokers are running, and turn any ad you find into a better one for you. That's the part that actually moves the needle.
